5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

1. Shared parser reads month first

The shared parser treats a date as MM/DD/YYYY. These tenants write the day before the month. When both numbers are at most 12, a date can land in another month instead of failing visibly. This affects app and email reports, and form reports from tenants whose form shows the day-first DD/MM/YYYY hint. The parser group is the cause that belongs to #4471.

Action: Make the parser use the tenant's date order, and test ambiguous dates in the affected locales. Check unambiguous dates too, so they fail clearly if their order is wrong.

Track the parser failure.

2. Booking form hint contradicts tenant date order

The booking-v3 form shows MM/DD/YYYY to people at day-first tenants. A person can follow that hint, but the form then reads the entered date in the tenant's day-first order. This is a form hint failure, distinct from the shared parser. It occurs on form reports for the affected booking-v3 tenants and needs its own ticket.

Action: Show the date format used to interpret the field, and test entry through the form in each tenant locale. Correcting the shared parser alone will not make this hint truthful.

Track the form hint failure.
